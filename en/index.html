Build a company around real production flow

ORVUNO is a persistent browser management game where business growth depends on complete operational chains. Purchasing, deliveries, storage, staff, machinery, production, packaging, customer demand and logistics all need to work together.

Play ORVUNO in your browser

The current game interface is primarily German.

Purchasing & storage

Source materials, consider delivery timing and keep enough warehouse capacity for incoming goods and finished production.

Production chains

Match materials, staff, machinery and capacity. More equipment only helps if the real bottleneck is actually the machine.

Orders & logistics

Work backwards from customer demand and delivery deadlines to plan materials, production, packaging and shipment.

Growth & resilience

Expand when the business can support the next step. Liquidity, capacity and throughput matter more than expansion for its own sake.
